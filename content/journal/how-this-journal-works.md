This journal is the public version of a discipline I'm trying to make permanent: **no position without a written thesis, and no thesis without its invalidation.**

## The rules

1. **Every entry states a horizon.** Days, months, or regime-length — but a number.
2. **Every entry carries sizing logic.** Not the size itself, but the reasoning that would produce one.
3. **Every entry names its kill criterion before it runs.** Written down, dated, non-negotiable.
4. **Closed entries don't get edited.** They move to Trade Aftermath and get the autopsy.
5. **Being wrong on schedule is a success condition.** A thesis that dies on its kill criterion worked exactly as designed.

## Why in public

Because private journals forgive. A public one doesn't. If the reasoning is embarrassing, better to find out from a stranger on the internet than from a drawdown.
