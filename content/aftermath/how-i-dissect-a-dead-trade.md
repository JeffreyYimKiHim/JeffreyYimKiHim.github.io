Every entry in this section follows the same six cuts. The format is the point: if a post-mortem can't fill all six, the original thesis was vaguer than it felt.

## 1. Hypothesis

One sentence, falsifiable. "X will happen within Y because Z" — not a narrative, a bet.

## 2. Expression

Why this instrument, this structure, this entry point. The best idea with the wrong expression is a donation to the market's option sellers.

## 3. Sizing

What fraction of risk budget, and why that number. Conviction sized, not felt.

## 4. What the market actually did

Facts only. No reinterpretation. The tape doesn't owe the thesis anything.

## 5. What was knowable at entry

The honest cut. Separating *bad luck* from *bad process* is the entire exercise. If the losing information was available and ignored, that's process. If it was genuinely unknowable, that's variance — and the correct response to variance is sizing, not regret.

## 6. Kill criteria for next time

What specific, observable condition would have exited this trade earlier — written so that future-me cannot negotiate with it.
