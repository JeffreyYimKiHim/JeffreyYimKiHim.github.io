> **Nature:** Positioning framework, not a set of point-entry trades

## 1. Macro Premise (verified as of mid-September 2026)

- Kevin Warsh is Fed Chair (sworn in May 2026). On **Sep 16, 2026 the Fed hiked 25bp to 3.75–4.00%**, unanimously — the first hike in over three years. The dot plot carries a median of one further hike in 2026 and a 2027 median of **4.1%**.
- Working scenario: roughly **three total hikes into mid-2027** — at the hawkish end of market pricing, but inside the priced distribution.
- **Critical nuance:** Warsh explicitly rejects forward guidance and did not submit his own dot. The "guidance" is market pricing, not a commitment; it can reprice violently on a single CPI or oil print.
- **Inflation is supply-shock-driven** (Middle East conflict, oil above $100, tariffs). Hikes into supply shocks compress multiples and demand without fixing the price level's source — a worse equity regime than growth-driven tightening.
- **The SNB is not following:** policy rate at **0%**, expected on hold through 2026 and likely into 2027. Fed→Swiss-equity transmission runs through **global risk appetite and USD/CHF, not local discount rates**.

## 2. Transmission Channels → Portfolio Implications

**A. Discount-rate compression (bearish high multiples).** The premium-duration names carry the most multiple risk: Givaudan (24.5x fwd P/E), Zurn Elkay (23.1x), Sika (21.4x). GIVN is doubly exposed — richest valuation *and* lowest implied upside (+5.6%).

**B. USD/CHF translation (quietly bullish Swiss exporters).** NESN, NOVN, GIVN, SIKA, HOLN earn heavily in USD; a stronger dollar inflates CHF-reported revenue — an earnings tailwind partially offsetting multiple compression. For USD-based buyers of CHF shares, the same move claws back return unless hedged — and hedging costs ~4% annualized in forward points given the rate gap, which alone consumes most of NESN/NOVN's implied upside. **The FX decision is unavoidable and must be made consciously.**

**C. Sector rate sensitivity.** Regional banks gain NII *if* the soft landing holds, but the tape shows regionals are among the first sold on hawkish surprises (funding costs, CRE credit). Reinsurers gain reinvestment yield — but SREN's tailwind is already priced (only name above consensus target, 11 sells). Construction-exposed industrials face energy-cost pressure with partial pricing-power offset.

## 5. After-thoughts / Review Notes

- **The list was already constructed better than the question assumed.** Mostly defensive Swiss quality sheltered by a 0% SNB, plus value-tilted US cyclicals — a reasonable posture for a supply-shock hiking cycle. The marginal value of the analysis was in the *tilts* (toward SUN/HOLN/HBAN, away from GIVN/SREN), not a wholesale rebuild.
- **Three facts did most of the work:** (1) the hike path is priced, not guided — Warsh abolished the calendar, so entry timing and staged allocation matter more than usual; (2) the inflation source is supply-side, which inverts the usual "hikes = fine for equities" heuristic; (3) the Fed–SNB divergence means the Swiss names' domestic discount rate is anchored — the cycle reaches them only through FX and global risk appetite. Everything else follows from these.
- **The FX hedge decision deserves its own line item.** A ~4% annualized hedging cost is large enough to change name selection on its own; it should be decided at the portfolio level before sizing, not discovered afterward.
- **Watch item for the next review:** whether consensus targets get cut on the premium names as the 2027 dot path firms. The +27% upsides on ZWS/SUN-type names embed pre-hike discount rates in many models; the value cluster's multiples have far less to give back.
- **Standing instruction:** this positioning is FOMC-conditional. Each meeting that adds (removes) a 2027 hike shifts names between tiers — re-underwrite the tiers on every SEP rather than letting the table go stale.

*Not investment advice. Macro positioning under active policy repricing carries elevated headline risk; tier assignments are conditional on the stated hike path and must be re-evaluated as the path reprices.*
